Key findings

You receive more than you send

1.32× more people reached out to you than you reached out to. In Feb–Mar 2026 at peak activity, you received 59 inbound vs sent 54 outbound — nearly equal, suggesting your posting drove reciprocal inbound interest.

High inbound acceptance rate: 89.7%

You accepted nearly 9 in 10 incoming requests. Only ~8 were declined or left pending, suggesting you have a broad, open networking posture.

The message paradox

Personalised messages had a lower acceptance rate (60.0% vs 79.6%). This isn't because messages hurt — it's because all 10 personalised messages were cold outreach to strangers (recruiters, hiring managers), a naturally harder audience. Blank requests mostly went to warm contacts.

Activity concentrated in Feb–Mar 2026

86% of all invitations in the period happened in just two months — perfectly overlapping with your peak posting months. This strongly suggests job-search intensity, not organic growth, drove the spike.
